01 About

Where I fit

I am useful when the product is too technical for generic marketing copy.

I come from a software engineering background, so I am comfortable sitting with architecture, infrastructure, security tradeoffs, workflow details, and the product logic behind the pitch.

My job is to turn that messy product knowledge into writing a smart buyer can trust: clear enough to move quickly, specific enough to survive technical scrutiny, and human enough that it does not sound like it was assembled in a content factory.

02 Process

How I work

I write from the argument outward.

01

Find the real claim

I look for the thing your product can say that a competitor cannot say without lying.

02

Keep the technical spine

I do not sand the details down until the piece becomes a vague promise with nicer spacing.

03

Make the buyer care

I connect the technical mechanism to risk, cost, speed, trust, and the decision someone has to make.
