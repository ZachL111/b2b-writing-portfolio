About

I make technical work sound as strong as it actually is.

I come from a software engineering background, so I do not need the simplified version of the product before I can write about it. I like the messy middle: architecture, security tradeoffs, buyer urgency, implementation detail, and the part where everyone knows what the product does but nobody has said it cleanly yet.

I turn complex technical ideas into content that works for practitioners and executives: white papers, thought leadership, solution briefs, case studies, technical blogs, editorial content, web copy, and executive explainers that do not read like committee paste.
